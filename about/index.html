Why we exist

Most early-stage SaaS companies run support from a shared inbox or a founder's phone. It works until customers grow, and then replies slow down, questions repeat and nobody is sure who owns what.

We are a boutique firm that fixes this with practical work: an audit to find the gaps, a helpdesk set up properly, SOPs and escalation paths written down, and part-time leadership while you are not ready to hire full-time.
