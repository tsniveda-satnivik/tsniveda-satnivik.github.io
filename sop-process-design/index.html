Questions

What is a support SOP?

A support SOP, or standard operating procedure, is a short written guide for handling a specific type of request the same way every time.

How many SOPs does a startup need?

Most early-stage teams need 8 to 12 for their most common request types. We choose the first set from your real ticket data.

What format are the documents in?

We deliver them in the tools you already use, such as Google Docs, Notion or Confluence, plus your helpdesk's knowledge base if you have one.

Does this work without a helpdesk?

Yes. SOPs and escalation paths work with any setup, including a shared inbox. They also make a later helpdesk setup much easier.
