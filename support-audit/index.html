Questions

What does a customer support audit include?

A review of your ticket flow and tools, a ranked list of the top five gaps, a quick wins document, and a 30/60/90-day roadmap as a PDF, plus a one-hour readout call.

How long does it take?

The written report is delivered 5 days after we have access to your support tools and data.

Which tools do you review?

Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, shared inboxes and most other helpdesk setups.

Do you need access to customer data?

We work with ticket metadata and workflow settings wherever possible. We agree access and handling rules with you before the audit starts.

What happens after the audit?

You can run the roadmap yourself, or we can implement it with you through helpdesk setup, process and SOP design, or fractional support leadership.
