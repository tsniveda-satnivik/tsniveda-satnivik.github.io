What we take on

The scope is agreed up front and adjusted as you grow. Typical areas:

Queue and priority review

A regular look at open tickets, ageing and backlog, with clear priorities for the week.

Process and tool ownership

We keep your helpdesk rules, saved replies and SOPs working as the product changes.

Hiring support

Role descriptions, interview scorecards and an onboarding plan for your first support hires.

Metrics and reporting

A short report on volume, response times and recurring problems, with what to do about them.

Coaching

Guidance for the founder or support team on tough tickets, tone and escalation decisions.

Customer feedback loop

Recurring issues turned into clear, prioritised input for product and engineering.

Who this is for

  • Founders who handle support themselves and want to step back from it
  • Startups with one or two support people and no one experienced to guide them
  • Teams that have had an audit or a setup and want someone to keep it running
  • Companies not ready for a full-time support manager

Questions

How is fractional support billed?

Engagements start with a one-time onboarding fee, then ongoing billing agreed in your proposal, such as per ticket or per chat. We confirm terms in writing before work starts.

How is this different from an agency or outsourced support team?

We do not replace your agents. We guide, set up and improve how your own team handles support.

Can this follow a support audit?

Yes, and it is a common path. The audit roadmap becomes the starting plan for the engagement.

Do you need access to customer data?

We work with ticket metadata and workflow settings wherever possible, and agree access and handling rules with you before we begin.
