What we set up

Most startups buy a helpdesk and leave it on its defaults. We configure it around your real workflow, then hand it over documented.

Workflow mapping

How requests arrive today, who owns what, and where tickets get stuck, before we touch a setting.

Channels and routing

Email, chat and forms connected, with rules that send each request to the right queue or person.

Saved replies and automations

Macros or canned responses for your common questions, plus triggers for tagging, priority and follow-up.

Priorities and SLAs

Response targets by request type or plan, with views and alerts so breaches are visible.

Reporting basics

A simple dashboard for volume, response time and backlog, so you can see how support is doing.

Handover and walkthrough

Written setup notes and a walkthrough call, so your team can run and adjust it without us.

Who this is for

  • Startups moving from a shared inbox to a real helpdesk
  • Teams already on Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom whose setup grew messy or was never finished
  • Founders about to hire their first support person who want a system ready for them
  • SaaS companies that promise different support levels by plan and need routing to match

Questions

Which helpdesk tools do you set up?

Zendesk, Freshdesk and Intercom. If you use something else, tell us on the discovery call and we will say honestly whether we are the right fit.

Can you move us off a shared inbox?

Yes. We plan the move so existing conversations and your team's habits carry over without a gap in customer replies.

Do you need access to customer data?

We work with workflow settings and ticket metadata wherever possible. We agree access and handling rules with you before any work starts.

Work is fixed-scope: we agree the deliverables and fee before we begin. A support audit first can help decide what is worth setting up.
